## Research Methodology

The analysis framework is based on:

1. Market Structure Analysis
Identifying trend conditions, structural shifts and important price areas.

2. Liquidity Analysis
Mapping areas where market participants may seek liquidity before expansion.

3. Price Action Observation
Studying reaction, rejection and continuation behavior around important zones.

4. Outcome Validation
Comparing the original thesis with actual market delivery after completion.
